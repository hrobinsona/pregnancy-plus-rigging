# Research basis for the code-character pipeline

Compiled 11 Oct 2026 from three focused research passes (industry cut-out rigging; animation craft; character
design for animation). Every rule carries a source. **[inf]** marks our own inference. Section **"→ pipeline"**
says what each finding changes in the engine, the character contract or the skill. Conflicts between sources (and
with the original prompt pack) are called out and resolved explicitly.

---

## 1. Cut-out rigging practice (Toon Boom Harmony, Moho, After Effects, Spine, Live2D)

**Structure**
- One drawing layer per part, each under its own peg; animate pegs, choose drawings on the layer [T1]. Minimum hierarchy: arms → torso, legs → hips, torso–neck–head chain, master peg on top [T1]; "linking and pegging everything to the hips" works for humanoids [T14]. Master peg between the feet [TB11].
- Pivots at the centre of each articulation [T2]; arm/forearm/hand pivots collinear, thigh/shank/foot collinear, head/neck/torso on one vertical axis [RL].
- Draw order is its own (keyable) channel, separate from hierarchy [T1].
- Build only what the animatic needs [T14]; test 2–3 special poses before calling a rig done [T12]; relaxed default pose, not a T-pose [T12].

**Joints**
- Joints are circles: the part behind gets a half-circle end overlapping the part in front [T2]; two overlapping circles never break [ADB]. Draw every piece complete; tracing parts exactly as on the model sheet leaves gaps [T8, TBstudy].
- "Patch": a trimmed copy of the front fill laid over the seam hides the joint line [T3, T4].
- Over-bending opens a gap; a round joint cannot go behind the part it overlaps [T2]. Deformer-driven limbs need no overlap [T2].
- Lineless (flat, no outline) characters are "much more forgiving" — parts can simply overlap [T15].
- Linear blend skinning loses volume in tight bends → extra elbow bone scaled by angle, or extend lower-limb art over the joint [S7].

**Deformers, correctives**
- Bone deformers for limbs/fingers; curve deformers for hair, mouths, brows, torsos, rubber-hose limbs; envelope deformers for cloaks/shoulders/chins and for faking a view change without a swap [T5]; envelopes are the main tool at Mercury Filmworks and Lighthouse [T16, T14].
- Moho smart bones = pose-space correctives: key the fully bent pose, check midway, fix shape by point edits; driven by the bone angle [M1 §7.5.3, M4]. Volume "muscle" bones driven by forearm angle ×0.5 [M1 §3.2]. Region binding beats "flexible" binding (rubbery cross-influence) [M1, M2].
- Angle limits per joint (e.g. forearm −10° to 100°) [M1 §3.2].

**Swaps and libraries**
- Hands are ONE layer with alternate poses (a library), not individually animated fingers — "looks less mechanical" [T8, T7]; shows have had "over a hundred" hand swaps, named with angle suffixes [T12].
- Mouths are swaps; eyes = eyeball + pupil + lid that clips the pupil; jaw separate [T8]. Hanna-Barbera/Preston Blair mouth set A–F (+G, H, X) [RH].
- Rigged path shapes need consistent vertex counts to interpolate [A9].

**Head turns, turnarounds**
- 5-view turnaround (front, ¾, profile, ¾ back, back) [T9]; front first, then ¾; landmarks line up across views [T12].
- Head turns as pose interpolation: joystick with 5 poses (origin, L, R, up, down) [A9, A11] or a 3×3 grid of 9 keyforms (Live2D, Angle X/Y ±30°) [L1–L3]; far side narrower, near side wider [L2]; layer-order switches between pairs (eyes/nose, ears/face) at set angles [L6]. Master-controller grids for head turns; mouths kept off them ("too organic") [T6, T15].

**IK, feet, walks**
- Feet: lock the foot while it is planted, unlock just before lift [M1 §3.2]; IK targets on the ground line, hips free [S8]; IK nails only hold on keys — constrain over the whole contact range [T10]. → solve per frame, never tween joint angles during contact [inf].
- IK softness / "realism" below 100% so limbs don't snap straight at full extension [S4, A5]; partial IK/FK mixes can flip the bend [S4].
- Spatial (path) interpolation must be curved or limbs travel in straight lines [A1].
- Duik walk generator: speed changes step amplitude not frequency; arms FK, feet IK; output is a starting point, not final animation [A2].

**Quality / floatiness**
- "Harmony is the world's stupidest in-betweener" — keys are the animator's job [T15]. Put cut-out on 2s for snappier shows [G1]. Draw the pose first, then match the rig to it [T16]. Use the rig for subtle motion and moving holds; draw smears, extreme stretches, foreshortened limbs [G1].
- Design for the rig: joints as circles; keep clothing details, patterns and seams off elbows/knees [T12, T13]; keep head, hands, feet separate from stretchy limbs [T17]; leave room for a big mouth [T13].

## 2. Animation craft (Williams, Thomas & Johnston, Lasseter, Goldberg, Hooks)

(30 fps = 24 fps frames × 1.25)
- **Walk poses per step:** Contact → Down (lowest, both knees bent, hands furthest apart) → Passing → Up (highest) → Contact [TBwalk]. Pick one scheme (some put the high point at Passing [CGG]).
- **Step timing (frames/step @30):** fast 10, natural 15, stroll 20, tired 25, heavy/slow 30 [MON, BLM]. Heavier = slower contacts (12s→16–18s @24) [LYN]. Loop: contacts at 1, 13, 25 (@24); never play the duplicate end frame [MON].
- Arms opposite legs; hips and shoulders counter-twist; mirror the half-cycles exactly or it reads as a limp [CGG].
- **Arcs:** interpolate path separately from timing; "straight inbetweens can completely kill the essence of an action"; faster = flatter arc [LAS87].
- **Slow in/out:** cluster in-betweens at extremes, "one fleeting drawing halfway"; timing charts with favour cushions [LAS87, BLMtc]. Spline overshoot on big changes → lock with duplicate keys [LAS87].
- **Number of in-betweens = meaning:** 0 hit by force, 1 hit by object, 2 twitch, 3 dodge, 4 crisp, 10 stretching a sore muscle [LAS87].
- **Overlap:** parts start/stop 1–3 frames apart; heavier parts drag further and settle later; never stop all parts on the same frame [LAS87, AM-hold].
- **Anticipation** scales with speed; slow actions need little [LAS87]. **Secondary action** stays subordinate; change expression before or after a big move, never during [LAS87, T&J p.64].
- **No twins**: never pose both sides identically; stagger sides in time [T&J p.67]. **Successive breaking of joints**: shoulder → elbow → wrist → fingers [BLMsb].
- **Staging**: one idea at a time, readable in silhouette [LAS87, Williams via AWN].
- **Eyes → head → body**, each leading by a few frames (longer for big decisions); externally driven motion reverses it (body first, head trails) [LAS94].
- Gaze within ~±18° is mostly eyes-only; larger shifts recruit the head [PMC4370616].
- **Blinks**: lid activity accompanies 97% of gaze shifts > 33° [SB-blink]; full closure at mid-turn, head on an arc, small settle [TUTS-head]. Close 4–6 f, brief hold, open 3–5 f (@30); upper lid leads, lower trails; offset brows and lids [AM-blink]. Rate: 17/min at rest (~every 105 f), 26/min talking, 4.5/min reading [PUB9399231]; randomise intervals; slow blinks = tired/sad, quick = nervous [AM-blink].
- **Eye darts**: no in-betweens or a single 1-frame cushion; they signal thought [AA-dart]. Thought beat ≈ hold + darts + blink → decision → eyes lead into action [inf from Hooks].
- **Hands**: break symmetry with offset timing; wrists lead; fingers settle at different times; arcs; no flat "spatula" hands [AM-hands].
- **Holds**: never dead; arrive slightly short of the extreme and drift into it; stagger when wrists/head/hips stop; keep eyes alive [AM-hold, ANIMWORKS, LAS94].

## 3. Pregnancy: posture, gait, breathing (physiology)

- **Gait, third trimester vs control** [BERTUIT15]: speed 0.99 vs 1.26 m/s; cadence 102 vs 112 steps/min; step length 0.58 vs 0.67 m; step width 10 vs 8 cm; double support 24% vs 17% of cycle; cycle 1.20 vs 1.07 s → **≈18 frames/step, 36-frame cycle, 4–5 f double support per contact @30** [inf].
- Meta-analysis: wider steps, shorter stride, more double support, slower speed, progressing with gestational week [LI24].
- **No waddle**: kinematics "remarkably unchanged", no waddling found; max anterior pelvic tilt +4° mean [FOTI00]; pelvic frontal-plane motion reduced [PMC4283355]; pelvis–thorax rotation less anti-phase, smaller (trunk moves more as a block) [VU-gait]. **Conflict with the prompt pack** ("lateral trunk sway, a gentle waddle") → we follow the measurements: modest sway; base width, longer contacts and slower cadence carry the read.
- **Posture**: lumbar extension up to 28° keeps the COM over the hips [WHITCOME07]; female lordosis spans 3 vertebrae [PUB18075592]; more anterior pelvic tilt late, more thoracic kyphosis, wider base [UCR, PMC4988898] → upper torso leans back; plumb line ear → ankle [inf].
- **Bump**: fundus at pubis 12–14 wk, navel 20–22 wk, bottom of the breastbone at 36 wk, drops at 37–40 wk (first pregnancy: 2–4 wk before labour) [FUNDAL, PAMPERS]. Abdominal depth +11.2 cm at 37 wk, hip breadth only +1.4 cm [DTIC98] → the front view barely widens; ¾ and profile carry the read. Egg-shaped, fuller lower curve [inf]. Belly is a heavy firm mass: drags 2–3 f behind the pelvis, low amplitude, no jiggle [LAS87 + inf].
- **Breathing**: rate unchanged or 15→17/min [PMC4818213, DERANGED] → 3.5–4 s per breath; **I:E ≈ 1:2 at rest** [DERANGED-IE]; tidal volume +30–50% [DERANGED]. Late pregnancy: rib-cage expansion reduced, diaphragm/abdominal contribution increased [LOMAURO19] (the 2015 review credits the rib cage — sources conflict; the 2019 primary data is newer). **Conflict with the prompt pack** ("inhale ~55%, exhale ~35%"; "shift more of the breath into the ribcage") and with our house rule "inhale longer than exhale" → for calm breathing we follow physiology: shorter active inhale, longer passive exhale, end-expiratory pause; lower-rib widening + belly rise, minimal shoulder lift. The pack shape remains available as a "deep/savouring breath" preset.

## 4. Character design for animation

- **Model-sheet package**: construction sheet (proportions in head units with landmark lines), head and body turnarounds, colour sheet (light/mid/dark per key colour), expression sheet (~6), pose sheet (4–5), hands and feet [CLIP, WIKI-MS, CGW]. Landmark heights live in one shared table; every view reads it [inf].
- **Proportions**: real women 7–7.5 heads; stylised standard ~6 heads, cartoony ~3 [TB-prop]; hand ≈ face length; foot ≈ forearm; face ≈ 5 eye-widths; fingertips at mid-thigh, elbows at waist, wrists at crotch [SCI]. Don't rely on a big head for appeal (Disney Research: enlarged heads rejected) [DISNEY-R]; avoid infantilising pregnant women [inf]. One defining feature [CB40].
- **Shape language**: straights against curves — one side of a form straight, the other curved [DROUJKO, MARTINS]; no parallels (tilt one) [DROUJKO]; no twins [EVL]; no tangents [ANIMISLAND]; big/medium/small contrast [SILVER via CB40]; one line of action (gentle S for relaxed poses) [TUTS-loa, MARTINS]; silhouette test with negative space, push 20% if unreadable [ANIMWORKS-sil]; interior folds echo silhouette shapes [VALVE08]; shape = personality [BTL Wolfwalkers].
- **Flat style**: few primitives, one shader per object [BUCK-mailchimp]; solid colour, no transparency, simple/no gradients [IBM]; no strokes, one skin tone, shadow ≈ 10% multiply, overlapping limb 10% lighter [ONS]; shadows go cool, not black; highest contrast at the focal point [VALVE08]; dot eyes and curve mouths, no pure black [ONS, HEADSPACE]. Four fingers; back of hand straight/angular, palm side curved; separate one finger from the silhouette; geometric hands, not mittens [MF, DROUJKO, CF]. Clothing: basic smooth shapes, minimal anchors, full garment drawn under overlaps [ONS].
- **Parts for rigs**: joints are circles, pivot at the circle centre [TB-fix, ADB]; pieces complete and overlapping [TB-study]; keep seams, stripes, cuffs and folds > 1 joint radius from any pivot [inf]; hand library ≈ 12 poses (relaxed, open, fist, point, grip, rest × palm/back) [inf from T7/T12].
- **Depicting pregnancy**: whole person with a face — headless bumps are "dehumanizing" [SCIAM, LAMAZE].

## 5. → pipeline (what this changes)

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| Hands are libraries; fingers settle at different times | Parametric hand generator with finger bones **plus** a JSON hand-pose library (relaxed, open, cup, flat, press, fist, point, grip; back/palm). Poses blend in finger-angle space; per-finger lags give staggered settles. |
| Joints are circles; pivots at circle centres, collinear | `limb()` caps are circles centred on the pivots; skeleton test checks collinearity at rest and cap coverage at min/max angles. |
| Smart-bone correctives | Angle-driven **shape-target correctives** (blend control-point offsets by joint angle), not just opacity swaps. Elbow, wrist, knee, hip (seated). |
| Head turns via joystick/grid; far side narrower | Head yaw/pitch channels drive a parametric face (features placed on a head ellipsoid), with layer-order switches; ±30° range. |
| Turnarounds are 5 views | Master = ¾ front. Other views are an extension path (view swaps in the skill), not built now. |
| Feet: lock during contact, solve every frame | Foot pins = the hand-pin mechanism generalised (plane-aware IK per frame). |
| Pregnant gait numbers; no waddle | Walk generator defaults: 18 f/step, 36 f cycle, 4–5 f double support, +25% base width, reduced pelvis–thorax twist, modest sway. Overrides the pack's "waddle". |
| I:E ≈ 1:2; abdominal + lower-rib breathing late in pregnancy | Breath shape default inhale 35% / exhale 55% / pause 10%; across-chest widening + belly rise, little shoulder lift. Overrides the pack and our Prompt C house rule; logged in DECISIONS. |
| Blinks 4–6 close / 3–5 open @30, every ~3.5 s, on >30° gaze shifts | Blink defaults updated; blink scheduling rule in the skill. |
| Cut-out on 2s reads less floaty | `exposure: "twos"` (poses stepped, camera/FX on 1s) available; compare both per shot. |
| Design checklist | Critic B gets the 12-item design checklist (below) in addition to its scores. |

### Design checklist (Critic B)
1. Landmark consistency across views ≤3% of height [inf]. 2. Head count in target (6–7 stylised woman); hand ≈ face. 3. Straight side vs curved side on every limb and the torso. 4. No parallel contours (±5° over >20% length) [inf]. 5. No tangents (overlap clearly or gap ≥2% of height) [inf]. 6. No twinning (limb angles differ ≥8°, shoulders/hips/head tilted) [inf]. 7. Silhouette reads; negative space; hands clear of the body. 8. Big/medium/small size hierarchy. 9. One base + one shade per material, no pure black, highest contrast at face/chest. 10. Simplicity: shape budget, one defining feature. 11. Rig-ready: circular overlapping joints, pivots at circle centres, collinear, complete pieces, no detail near a joint. 12. Pregnancy: whole person with a face, bump stage matches fundal landmarks, lordosis + pelvic tilt + wider stance.
